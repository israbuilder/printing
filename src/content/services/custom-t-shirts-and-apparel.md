### Our Custom Apparel Methods

#### 1. Direct-to-Film (DTF) Printing
DTF is the modern standard for vibrant, multi-color graphics:
- **Full Color & Gradients:** Photo-realistic reproduction without color limitations.
- **Ultra Soft Hand:** Flexible, breathable, and stretches with the fabric without cracking.
- **All Fabric Types:** Perfect on 100% cotton, polyester, blends, canvas, and nylon.
- **Zero Minimums:** Cost-effective for 1 piece or 500 pieces.

#### 2. Screen Printing
The time-tested choice for high-volume corporate and promotional runs:
- **Cost-Effective for Bulk:** Significant volume discounts for orders of 50+ units.
- **Vibrant Spot Colors:** Pantone color matching for exact corporate branding.
- **Heavy-Duty Durability:** Inks cure deep into garment fibers for years of wear.

#### 3. Embroidery & Custom Patches
For an elevated, textured executive look:
- **Polos & Jackets:** Ideal for corporate office wear and trade show uniforms.
- **Snapbacks & Beanies:** 3D puff and flat embroidery for hats and outerwear.
