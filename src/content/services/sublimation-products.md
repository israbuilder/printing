## Custom Sublimation Printing & Personalized Gifts in Houston

Looking for vivid, permanent, photo-quality custom merchandise? **Albran Printing** specializes in dye-sublimation printing on a wide array of drinkware, accessories, and gifts.

Unlike surface decals or stickers that can peel over time, sublimation infuses the ink directly into the coating of the product. The graphic becomes an integral part of the item, resulting in a smooth finish that won't fade, scratch, or wash off.
