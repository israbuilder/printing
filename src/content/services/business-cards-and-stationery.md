## Premium Business Cards & Marketing Stationery in Houston

First impressions matter in business. When you hand a prospect a business card or marketing brochure from **Albran Printing**, they feel the quality immediately.

We combine heavy-weight premium cardstocks, razor-sharp offset and digital printing, and luxury finishes that set your brand apart in competitive industries.

### Our Paper & Print Products

#### 1. Premium Business Cards
- **16pt Heavy Cover Stock:** Solid, substantial card weight with your choice of High Gloss UV, Matte, or Silk finish.
- **Raised Spot UV & Soft-Touch Velvet:** Suede-like tactile texture combined with gleaming raised gloss over logos and text.
- **Foil Accent Cards:** Gleaming gold, silver, rose gold, or holographic foil stamped on premium matte backgrounds.
- **Ultra-Thick 32pt Trifecta / Color Core:** Eye-catching colored center layer sandwiched between two thick outer cards.
- **Waterproof Plastic Cards:** Frost, clear, and white plastic cards that won't tear or get soggy.

#### 2. Marketing Collateral & Flyers
- **Sales Flyers & Club Handouts:** 100lb Gloss Book or 100lb Gloss Cover in standard sizes (4x6, 5x7, 8.5x11).
- **Brochures & Menus:** Half-Fold, Tri-Fold, Z-Fold, and Gate-Fold with score lines that prevent paper cracking.
- **Postcards & Mailers:** Direct mail ready postcards with EDDM (Every Door Direct Mail) sizing compliance.
- **Presentation Folders:** 9x12 pocket folders with business card slits for corporate presentations and proposals.

#### 3. Corporate Stationery
- **Branded Letterhead & Envelopes:** 70lb Uncoated Linen and Smooth paper compatible with laser and inkjet printers.
- **Carbonless NCR Forms:** 2-part and 3-part invoice forms, work orders, and carbonless receipt pads.
- **Custom Notepads & Bookmarks:** Personalized desk pads, invoice slips, and event bookmarks.
