## Turn Your Vehicle into a 24/7 Mobile Billboard in Houston

With thousands of daily drivers commuting on I-45, I-10, Loop 610, and Highway 290, vehicle graphics offer the lowest cost per impression of any local advertising medium.

**Albran Printing** designs, prints, and installs durable, high-visibility commercial vehicle wraps, van graphics, truck lettering, and magnetic signs that generate calls wherever you drive.

### Vehicle Graphic Options

#### 1. Full & Partial Vehicle Wraps
- **Full Wraps:** Complete bumper-to-bumper transformation that commands maximum attention.
- **Partial Wraps:** Strategic branding across the rear doors, tailgate, and side panels for high impact at a budget-friendly price point.
- **Air-Release 3M Vinyl:** Eliminates air bubbles and conforms perfectly over vehicle curves and rivets.

#### 2. Commercial Van & Truck Lettering
- **Die-Cut Spot Graphics:** Clean vinyl cut logos, company name, contact info, and licensing numbers (TDLR, DOT, etc.).
- **Tailgate & Back Window Graphics:** Clear calls-to-action placed right at eye level for drivers behind you in traffic.
- **Reflective Safety Vinyl:** High-intensity reflective striping and lettering for utility, towing, and night-shift vehicles.

#### 3. Perforated Window Film (Window Perf)
- **One-Way Vision Vinyl:** 50/50 and 60/40 micro-perforated film allows full color graphic display on the outside while allowing clear visibility from the driver's interior cabin.
- **UV & Glare Reduction:** Acts as a sunshade while advertising your business.

#### 4. Heavy-Duty Magnetic Vehicle Signs
- **30 mil Vehicle-Grade Magnetic Sheeting:** Tested for highway speeds with rounded safety corners that prevent wind lift.
- **Easy On / Easy Off:** Ideal for sub-contractors, HOA restricted neighborhoods, and multi-purpose personal trucks.
