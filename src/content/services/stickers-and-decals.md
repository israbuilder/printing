## Custom Stickers, Labels & Vinyl Decals in Houston

Stickers are one of the most cost-effective and engaging ways to promote your brand, label your products, or express your creativity. **Albran Printing** produces premium-quality, high-resolution vinyl stickers and labels tailored to your exact specifications.

Every sticker is printed with rich, vibrant inks and coated with a protective weather-resistant laminate, ensuring they withstand outdoor exposure, water, scratches, and everyday handling.

### Sticker & Decal Styles

#### 1. Die-Cut Custom Vinyl Stickers
- **Custom Contour Cut:** Precision blade-cut to the exact silhouette of your logo or illustration.
- **Thick 6 mil Vinyl:** Heavyweight feel with strong, residue-free adhesive backing.
- **Matte or Gloss Lamination:** Choose soft silky matte or vibrant reflective gloss finish.

#### 2. Specialized Finishes & Materials
- **Holographic Stickers:** Mesmerizing rainbow spectrum reflections that shift under light.
- **Clear Transparent Vinyl:** Transparent background where only your artwork is printed, ideal for windows and jars.
- **Metallic & Chrome Foil:** Brushed aluminum, chrome, and gold effects for premium packaging.
- **Glow in the Dark:** Luminescent vinyl that charges under light and glows in darkness.

#### 3. Product & Packaging Labels
- **Roll Labels & Sheet Labels:** Efficient application for bottles, jars, cosmetic packaging, bakery boxes, and pouches.
- **Waterproof & Oil-Resistant:** Specialized polypropylene film resistant to moisture and refrigeration condensation.
- **Barcodes & QR Codes:** High-contrast crisp scanning reliability.

#### 4. Transfer Decals & Window Lettering
- **Single-Color Cut Vinyl:** Crisp lettering and logos pre-masked with transfer tape for storefront windows and vehicles.
- **Perforated One-Way Vision Vinyl:** Full-color graphics on the outside while maintaining full visibility from the inside.
