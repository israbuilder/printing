## High-Impact Custom Banners & Signage in Houston

Make a powerful visual statement with large-format banners and signs from **Albran Printing**. From grand openings and real estate campaigns to trade show exhibitions and construction sites, our custom signage turns passersby into customers.

We utilize wide-format digital printers with UV-resistant inks that deliver saturated, vibrant colors and sharp legibility from any distance.

### Signage & Display Solutions

#### 1. Outdoor Vinyl Banners
- **13 oz Heavy-Duty Scrim Vinyl:** The industry standard for vibrant indoor and outdoor promotions.
- **18 oz Blockout Vinyl:** Super-heavy banner material designed for double-sided outdoor display without show-through.
- **Mesh Vinyl Banners:** Micro-perforated material that lets air pass through, perfect for fences, sports fields, and high-wind Texas conditions.
- **Finishing Options:** Reinforced welded hem edges, pole pockets, brass grommets every 2 feet, and rope attachments.

#### 2. Corrugated Yard Signs (Coroplast)
- **18" x 24" Lawn Signs:** The most popular choice for contractors, roofers, landscapers, political campaigns, and real estate.
- **Fluted 4mm & 10mm Plastic:** Lightweight, waterproof, and paired with sturdy steel H-wire stakes.
- **Single or Double Sided:** Crisp full-color graphics on both sides for two-way traffic visibility.

#### 3. Trade Show & Indoor Displays
- **Retractable Pop-Up Banners (Roll-Ups):** Portable aluminum banner stands with carrying case; setup takes under 30 seconds.
- **Foam Board & PVC (Sintra) Signs:** Lightweight rigid boards for indoor presentations, easel displays, and menu boards.
- **A-Frame Sidewalk Signs:** Sturdy outdoor plastic sandwich boards with quick-change sign inserts for daily specials.

#### 4. Acrylic & Metal Storefront Signs
- **Brushed Aluminum & DiBond Signs:** Sleek commercial building signs for office lobbies and parking spaces.
- **Clear Acrylic Lobby Signs:** Modern standoff mounted acrylic plaques with polished flame edges.
